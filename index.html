How I work

  • Test the maths, not just the pageChecking my own worksheet grader turned up 716 wrong answers it was accepting. It now accepts none, and a weekly check runs against the live sites.
  • Design with the student, not for themMy son is the client for the kids' tools. One sentence from him redirected a design I'd spent five versions on.
  • Ship on a teacher's scheduleEach fix becomes a one-line step that I can run and verify from my phone, so work gets live in the evening between grading and bedtime.
